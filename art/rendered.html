The Rendered 100 — the best game worlds, rebuilt in 3D

The companion to The 100 — Best Game Screens: for each of the 100 most iconic game worlds, the best fan-made 3D version of it — dioramas, voxel builds and full level recreations, mostly from Sketchfab. Tap any render to open the real 3D model. 5 of 100 found so far — the hunt continues in batches.

Every model belongs to its creator, credited on each card, and links to its Sketchfab page. Renders shown are the creators' own preview images. Companion data: rendered100.json.
