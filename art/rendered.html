The Rendered 100 — the best game worlds, rebuilt in 3D

A hall of fame in its own right: the 100 best fan-made 3D versions of iconic game worlds — dioramas, voxel builds and full level recreations, mostly from Sketchfab. Every card is a real 3D render (no flat screenshots). Tap any render to open the actual 3D model. 7 of 100 so far — new batches land as they're verified. Companion: The 100 — Best Game Screens.

Every model belongs to its creator, credited on each card, and links to its Sketchfab page. Renders shown are the creators' own preview images. Companion data: rendered100.json.
