The Prop 100 — the best single objects, in 3D

The sourcing gallery for Prop Smith: 100 eyeball-verified single-object props from Sketchfab — a gnome, a treasure chest, a kart, a chair — picked because one object at a time is what our Qwen converts into game-ready Three.js props. No rooms, no landscapes, no character models: every card is exactly ONE thing. 100 of 100 verified (thumbnail and model page checked). 96 of the 100 are ⚒ Prop-Smith ready — CC-licensed and downloadable, so they're cleared for the conversion queue; the rest are gallery-only on license.
